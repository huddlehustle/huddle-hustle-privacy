Huddle Hustle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Huddle Hustle (the "Game") collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Game on Android and iOS and to this website.

The short version

  • You can play without creating an account or giving us your name or email. We never ask for them.
  • We store your game progress and gameplay data with our backend provider, Microsoft PlayFab, so the Game works across sessions and devices and so we can improve it.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, ads are only personalised if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play and the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with our charity partner ASOC.
  • The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data or to delete it at any time: penguinrescuegame@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the Game needs to work, to be improved and to fund itself. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

2.1 Game account and identifiers

  • Player ID: when you first open the Game, our backend provider, PlayFab, creates an account with a random player ID. The account is linked to an identifier generated for your installation of the Game.
  • Google Play Games or Game Center (optional): if you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), the Game uses your player ID from that service to sign you in. This keeps your progress if you change or reinstall your device. You can play without signing in.
  • Leaderboard name: if you take part in leaderboards (such as the Daily Expedition), your score is shown to other players together with your player name.

2.2 Game progress

Your total of Rescued Penguins, high score, unlocked Milestones and Traits, tutorial progress and the results of each run (for example penguins rescued and score). Some of this is saved on your device so you can play offline, and is synced to our servers when you are online.

2.3 Gameplay and device data

  • Gameplay events: how you play, such as session start and length, runs played, how and where runs end, and which features you use.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system and version, Game version, language, and error or crash information.
  • IP address: received by our servers and providers whenever the Game connects to the internet. It is used to deliver the service and may be used to work out your approximate country. We do not use it to find your precise location.

2.4 Feedback you send

If you use the in-game feedback option, we receive the text you write along with your player ID, the Game version and basic device information. Please don't include personal information such as your name, email address or phone number in your feedback.

2.5 Purchases

If you buy the Rescue Pass, Google Play or the Apple App Store processes the payment. We receive the purchase details we need to check that the purchase is genuine and to give you the Pass: the product, the order or transaction ID, a purchase receipt or token, the platform and the time of purchase. We also record which share of the Pass proceeds your Milestones have unlocked for ASOC (see section 5). We never receive or store your card or payment details.

2.6 Advertising data

If the Game shows you ads, Google AdMob collects information such as your device's advertising identifier (if available and allowed), IP address, device information and how you interact with ads. See section 4. If you own the Rescue Pass, the Game shows no interstitial ads, and rewarded ads are never required.

4. Ads and your choices

Huddle Hustle is free to play, and ads help fund it. The ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are personalised, the Game asks for your consent using Google's consent message. If you don't agree, you may still see ads, but they won't be based on your personal data. You can change your choice at any time in the Game's settings under Privacy.
  • iOS: the Game only accesses your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects for advertising. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

5. The Rescue Pass and ASOC

With the Rescue Pass, a share of the Pass proceeds goes to the Antarctic and Southern Ocean Coalition (ASOC). The share grows as you reach big Milestones in the Game, up to the maximum shown on the purchase screen before you buy. A share you haven't unlocked doesn't go to ASOC.

To calculate this, we keep a record that links each purchase (order ID and platform) to your player ID and to the share your Milestones have unlocked. We use it to work out the amount owed to ASOC, to publish totals, and for our accounts.

ASOC does not receive any personal information about you. We only report totals to ASOC, never individual players or purchases.

8. How long we keep it

  • Game account and progress: for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Gameplay events and feedback: only as long as we need them to analyse and improve the Game. After that they are deleted or aggregated so they no longer identify you.
  • Purchase and ASOC share records: for as long as Bulgarian and EU accounting and tax law requires. These records are kept even if you delete your account, but they are then no longer linked to your game progress.
  • Messages to us: for as long as we need to deal with your request, and afterwards only where we need a record of it, for example to show that we handled a privacy request.

Data saved on your device stays there until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, and in all cases if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access your data and get a copy of it;
  • Correct data that is wrong;
  • Delete your data (see section 10);
  • Restrict how we use it, or object to uses based on our legitimate interests;
  • Port your data: receive it in a common machine-readable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example for personalised ads, without affecting what happened before.

To use any of these rights, email penguinrescuegame@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. We may ask for information to confirm the request is yours, for example details only the account holder would know.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592). You can also contact the authority in the country where you live or work. We'd appreciate the chance to sort out your concern first.

10. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your Huddle Hustle account and the data linked to it at any time:

  1. Email penguinrescuegame@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion".
  2. Tell us which platform you play on (Android or iOS) and, if you can, your player ID or the Google Play Games or Game Center name you play with.
  3. We confirm the request, delete your account, progress, gameplay events and feedback within 30 days, and let you know when it's done.

Deletion is permanent: your Rescued Penguins, Milestones and Traits cannot be restored. Records we must keep by law (see section 8) are kept until their legal retention period ends. A Rescue Pass you bought stays tied to your Google Play or App Store account, so you can restore it later. To also remove the data saved on your device, uninstall the Game.

11. Children

Huddle Hustle is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, please contact us and we will delete their data.

The categories of information we collect, and why, are described in sections 2 and 3. To make a request, email penguinrescuegame@gmail.com.

13. Security

Data is sent between the Game and our servers over encrypted connections. Only server functions we control can change your progress and purchase records, and access to our backend is limited to the team members who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

We update this policy when the Game or the law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the Game before it takes effect and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again.
